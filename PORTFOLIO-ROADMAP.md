## Boundaries and collaboration

- **matematications:** understand, derive, implement and validate mathematical methods. Keep one canonical study for each method, including method-focused experiments and paper reproductions.
- **metron:** evaluate competing models under shared, reproducible protocols. Keep comparative research questions, model adapters and evaluation evidence here.
- **conjectura:** investigate published mathematical conjectures with AI-assisted exploration, proof search and rigorous verification. Follow the project's own research and publication policies; do not impose a quota of solved conjectures.
- **chuchuva:** explore rain without disciplinary restrictions or a fixed completion date. Use methods appropriate to each question, from mathematical experiments to historical and cultural studies.
- **Applied projects and academic research:** own their domain-specific implementations, data and findings. Link reusable studies rather than duplicating them.

Consult those sources for current status rather than treating this roadmap as a live results record.

## Learning protocol

Problem → prerequisite map → targeted reading → hand-worked example → implementation → validation → transfer exercise → technical note.

For computational studies, include the question, assumptions, references, reproducible instructions, baseline, tests and limitations. For pure mathematical explorations, emphasize definitions, proof, examples and counterexamples. For interdisciplinary work, use appropriate disciplinary methods rather than forcing a computational framework.

Record AI assistance. Distinguish proposed work, executed work, verified results and independent understanding.

## Public release checklist

- Clear research question and scope.
- Evidence appropriate to the type of work, with provenance and uncertainty where applicable.
- Reproduction instructions and tests for computational work.
- Known limitations and alternative explanations.
- Appropriate licensing, attribution and disclosure of AI assistance.
- No credentials, restricted data or unverified claims.

Keep the bilingual profile and repository READMEs consistent. Maintain links to canonical sources and avoid creating repositories merely to fill profile pins.
